**User**

It’s strange: when a pop-up window is open on the "Flow" tab, clicking on the chart doesn't close it, whereas clicking outside the chart does. 
This contradicts the behavior of other pop-up windows, where a click outside the window should close it (which is correct).

---

**Cursor**

I'll look at how Flow-tab popovers handle outside clicks compared with the other diagram popups, and why a click on the chart itself doesn't dismiss them.

The chart stops `mousedown` before it reaches the document, so the open popup treats that press as cancelled and stays open. A click outside the chart still bubbles, which is why only that one closes it. I'll make a press on the chart dismiss the popup the same way.

A press on the Flow chart was stopping before it reached the page, so an open popup treated that press as cancelled and stayed open. A click on the editor or the rest of the page still bubbles, which is why those clicks already closed the popup.

The chart does this on purpose: the background drag captures the press, and React Flow's drag and zoom do the same on nodes and the pane. The fix replays a normal bubbling click when that press starts on the chart and a popup is open, before the chart swallows the original press. The chart still gets the real press, so selecting a node still works. A click inside the popup is left alone.

I checked this in the browser. Clicking the empty chart or a node closes Flow options and the export menu. Clicking a checkbox inside Flow options keeps the popup open.
